# Use TLÁO Mail in your browser

## 1. Open webmail

Open the secure address issued for your mailbox:

```text
https://<webmail-host>
```

The documentation site is a guide; it is not the mailbox sign-in page. Your
webmail address may differ from your email domain.

## 2. Sign in

1. Enter your **Email** or the legacy mailbox login issued by your administrator.
2. Enter your main mailbox password in **Passphrase**.
3. Select **Remember Me** only on a device you control.
4. Choose **Sign in**. If prompted, enter the current six-digit code from your authenticator.

A remembered login does not replace knowing your password. Sign out when
finished on a shared device.

## 3. Read and send mail

Select **Inbox** and open a message to read it. Use **Reply** to answer the
sender, or **Reply all** when everyone on the message should receive the response.

To send a new message:

1. Choose **New message**.
2. Enter the recipient, subject, and message.
3. Add attachments if needed, then check the recipient and files.
4. Choose **Send** and look in **Sent** for your sent copy.

If sending fails, keep the draft and use the [troubleshooting guide](troubleshooting.md).
A message appearing in Sent confirms submission; it does not guarantee final delivery.

## 4. Find settings

Open the account menu at the top right and choose **Settings**. The settings
navigation includes **General**, **Accounts**, **Security**, and **Folders**.
Use General for layout and reading preferences, and Folders for folder mapping.

**Change password** is also available in the settings navigation. It opens
the account portal and asks for your current main password.

## Can't sign in?

Choose **Forgot password?** on the login page. The recovery guide explains
how your administrator verifies ownership and gives you a one-time reset link.
Read [password changes and recovery](passwords.md) before requesting help.

## Interface changes in this release

Login labels and buttons have stronger contrast. Settings content appears on
an opaque, readable panel, and the login footer no longer overlaps the form
on short screens. Reload an already-open tab to receive the updated interface.
